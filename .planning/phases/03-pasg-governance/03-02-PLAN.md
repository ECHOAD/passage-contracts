# Phase 03-02 - Weighted Voting, Delegation, And Threshold Rules

## Objective

Implement the actual PASG-holder voting mechanics inside the new `pasg-governance` contract: native `upasg` voting power, proposal-time snapshots, delegation, quorum, approval thresholds, and the guards that make vote outcomes deterministic before they can flow into the multisig bridge.

This wave must keep the execution/admin split introduced in 03-01. The work here is about governance power, not about shifting ownership away from `multisig`.

## Must Haves

### Truths

- PASG governance voting power is measured in native `upasg` units or a clearly documented escrowed representation of native `upasg`; it is not derived from multisig membership.
- Proposal outcomes are determined by on-chain threshold, quorum, and approval rules stored in `pasg-governance`.
- Delegation is explicit, queryable, and bounded so proposals remain deterministic during an open voting window.
- Execution remains scoped to PASG and protocol parameters; this wave must not introduce governance control over off-chain Passage business systems.

### Artifacts

- `contracts/core/pasg-governance/src/contract.rs` implements deposit, withdrawal, delegation, voting, and proposal-close logic.
- `contracts/core/pasg-governance/src/state.rs` stores voting balances, delegation relationships, proposal snapshots, and counted ballots.
- `contracts/core/pasg-governance/src/msg.rs` exposes query responses for config, voting power, delegation, proposals, ballots, and execution readiness.
- `contracts/core/pasg-governance/src/tests.rs` proves weighted voting, quorum, approval, delegation, and locked-window invariants.
