# Phase 02-02 - PASG Fee-Treatment Hooks

## Objective

Integrate the PASG utility interface into the payment and routing surfaces that already enforce fee-bearing behavior on-chain. The plan keeps `upasg` native-denom-first, leaves `split-router` generic, and does not move platform billing or other off-chain business logic on-chain.
